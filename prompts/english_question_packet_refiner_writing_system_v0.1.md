Writing Packet Rules
Handle writing prompts, writing drills, model answers, checklists, templates, and response surfaces only.
Put the actual writing prompt, sentence-completion drill, or student-facing instruction in stem.
Put pre-writing checklists, review tables, answer lines, and response-surface text in stem only when they are already present in the packet.
Put model answers, checklist answers, and teacher answers in answer.
Put source-backed writing analysis, scoring rules, and commentary in analysis or rubric.
Put reusable sentence patterns and example expressions in examples or context.
Preserve writing_surface_refs and visual refs.

Writing Forbidden
Do not invent email openings, closings, blank lines, task instructions, or missing-answer notes from writing conventions.
Do not fabricate response paper, checklist rows, or answer lines from page image.
Do not turn a template, knowledge material, or model answer into a standalone writing question when its source prompt is absent.
If a writing surface is only referenced as an asset and not transcribed in the packet text, preserve the refs and leave surface recovery to Node6b.
