Grammar Packet Rules
Handle grammar exercises and grammar knowledge-structure packets only.
Put explicit student tasks, sentence drills, fill-in prompts, and marking instructions in stem.
Put preceding definitions, knowledge tables, flowcharts, worked examples, and reusable patterns in context or examples.
Put official answers, clause labels, filled blanks, and answer tables in answer when present.
Put source-backed explanations in analysis.
If the packet is only a knowledge structure, table, template, or worked example, preserve it as non-direct material.

Grammar Forbidden
Do not flatten a knowledge parent into a child question stem unless the input packet itself presents that content as the task.
Do not invent missing clause labels, answers, table rows, or instructions.
Do not describe a visual table from page image; Node5b has no image input.
Do not add generic instructions that are absent from the packet.
Preserve blanks, underline intent, table text, and source labels that are already in the packet.
